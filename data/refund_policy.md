# Refund Policy

## Damaged Items
Customers may request a refund for items damaged during delivery if the damage is reported within 7 days of delivery.

The customer should provide the order ID and a description of the damage. Photos may be requested by a support agent when needed.

## Refund Processing
Once a refund is approved, the refund is initiated to the original payment method.

Refunds typically take 5-7 business days to appear after approval. The exact timing depends on the customer's bank or payment provider.

## Refund Approval
Refund requests above Rs. 1,000 require approval from a support agent before the refund can be issued.

Requests of Rs. 1,000 or less may be processed according to the applicable refund policy without additional agent approval.

## Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds are generally not available for:
- Items damaged after delivery due to customer misuse.
- Items returned outside the applicable return window.
- Final-sale items explicitly marked as non-refundable.

## Escalation
If a refund request cannot be resolved using this policy, the customer may request a support ticket or callback with a support agent.
