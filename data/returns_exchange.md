# Returns and Exchanges

## Return Window
Unopened items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Exchanges
Customers may request an exchange for a different size when the requested replacement is available.

Exchange requests should include the order ID and the desired replacement size.

## Eligibility
Items that have been used, damaged through customer misuse, or marked as final sale may not qualify for return or exchange.

## Shipping
Customers may be responsible for return shipping unless the item was defective, damaged during delivery, or the wrong item was shipped.
